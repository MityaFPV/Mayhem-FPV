# Changelog

## v0.7.0 (open beta)

First public release.

- Gamemode with its own namespace; other addons' cameras, HUDs and keybinds pause while you fly
- MayhemPhys 2.0: blade-element props, rotor wake, frame aerodynamics, real gyro, Betaflight 4.4 filters
- Betaflight flight controller, rates, CLI import, autotune
- Radios over raw HID (all 8 channels), Xbox / PlayStation gamepads, keyboard
- Betaflight analog OSD with the standard font and crosshair, layout editor, post-flight stats
- Analog video and radio link, motor sound, crash damage, turtle mode, LiPo battery model
- Detailed Mayhem X5 model with low-profile motors, propeller and action camera options
- Music player with your own folder, cinematic mode, walking with head bob, factory reset
